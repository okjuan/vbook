### building the midfield around Rice

In July of 2023 Arsenal bought Declan Rice from West Ham for a hefty sum, by far the highest price they've ever paid to transfer in a player from another club.
Ever since then Rice has been a fixture in the team.
But it's not because the manager Mikel Arteta is susceptible to the sunkcost fallacy.
Since taking the job in December of 2019 he has recruited and scrapped plenty players: Tavares, Lokonga, Ramsdale, Vieira, Kiwior, Zinchenko, and Nørgaard.
He can be ruthless.
He gave Nørgaard little opportunity.
He retracted his trust in Zinchenko.
He replaced Ramsdale, a decision that was unexpected and controversial but ultimately vindicated.

Rice did not integrate seamlessly into the team.
But Arteta persevered because Rice's qualities far exceed his flaws.
He gallops up and down and across the field dispossesing the opposition and disarming their offense.
And he does it seemingly _every_ game.
He performs so consistently.

Because of this, Arteta tailors the team to compensate for Rice's weaknesses.
He moved Rice up from the lone pivot #6 position to the leftsided #8 position (Xhaka's old position) and put Jorginho as the #6 instead.
This produced results immediately.
Rice was set free to roam and released of the responsibility of playing risky passes forward, something Jorginho did much more effectively.

This, however, posed a new problem.
Rice does not have Xhaka's creativity or craftiness.
He can strike the ball very well and he is a decent finisher but he is rarely afforded the space he needs to exploit this ability because Arteta prescribes slow progression into the final third and systematic siege of the opponent's penalty box.
Rice does not thrive when an intricate attack needs to be deployed because he is an athlete of power and competence and reliability, not a player of subtlety and guile and trickiness.
It should not be a surprise that the same player who in the defensive half struggles to wriggle out of tight spaces or make precise passes through them also struggles to do it in the final third.
